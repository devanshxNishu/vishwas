## Rule 2 — the one partial

The deck is prose-inside-cards rather than terse bullets. Mitigating factors:
every one of the 12 selected reference decks reviewed is equally dense, and the
deck now carries four visuals — the six-stage pipeline diagram, and figures on
slides 2, 3 and 4. Rule 2 says "try to avoid", not "must not". Judged
acceptable; if a shorter deck is ever wanted, the card bodies are where to cut.

their labels unchanged.**

## One thing that was removed for compliance

An interdisciplinary team block (six members with their disciplines) was
briefly placed on slide 1. **All 14 reference title slides show only the six
template fields — not one lists individual members.** Rather than take the
risk, it moved to slide 6, whose own prompt is "Details / Links of the
reference and research work" — attributing the research to the people who did
it is on-prompt there. The interdisciplinary story survives, the deviation does
not.
